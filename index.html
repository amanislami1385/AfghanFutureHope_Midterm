WHO WE ARE

Education should not depend on where you were born.

Afghan Future Hope is focused on helping Afghan youth discover educational and professional opportunities. We believe that access to information, skills and mentorship can help young people build stronger futures.

Our goal is simple: connect motivated students with scholarships, learning resources, technology and international opportunities.
